# 0004 — AI Budget

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Approved by:** Project owner

---

## Context

AI calls have a per-use cost. The project's stated budget is R$ 0 per month where possible, with a possible future budget of R$ 100 to R$ 200 per month (`CLAUDE.md`, section 8). The monthly limit for AI calls had not been set.

## Alternatives

1. **R$ 0 for now.** No paid AI calls until a budget is approved.
2. **Up to R$ 20 per month**, for controlled experiments.
3. **Up to R$ 100 per month**, which consumes much of the future budget.

## Decision

The monthly limit for AI calls is R$ 0 for now. No paid AI calls are made until a budget is approved.

## Rationale

The first AI-enabled version (Phase 6) is far away, and the project has no approved budget for it. A zero limit prevents unplanned cost and keeps the decision explicit.

## Consequences

- Development of Phase 6 may proceed with deterministic stubs or local test data until a budget is approved.
- Any change to this limit requires a new decision record.

# 0005 — Admission Model

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Approved by:** Project owner

---

## Context

`01-PRODUCT.md` (section 11, question 1) and `03-USER-EXPERIENCE.md` (section 4) left open how participants join an organization: freely, or by applying.

## Alternatives

1. **Direct role selection.** The participant chooses any available role.
2. **Application to predefined vacancies.** The organization publishes vacancies, and participants apply to them, simulating a hiring process.
3. **Full application and approval flow**, with interviews and decisions by simulated managers.

## Decision

Participants apply to predefined vacancies, which simulate a hiring process.

## Rationale

This gives the experience of joining a real company without the cost of a full recruitment flow. It also gives the simulation a natural source of work: open vacancies.

## Consequences

- `03-USER-EXPERIENCE.md` section 4 refers to this decision.
- The vacancy and application flow must be specified in the user experience and product documents when the phase that needs it is reached.
